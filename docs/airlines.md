# Airlines

Every airline publishes its change and cancellation rules differently. This page records what each airline's official website showed when checked (October and November 2026). Rules change often: always check the airline's current page.

The page and the PDF disagree on Value cancellation |

| **IndiGo (6E)** | [Fees and charges](https://www.goindigo.in/information/fees-and-charges.html) | Depends on the fare type (below) |

### IndiGo in detail

| Fare | How fees are set | Zones needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saver (regular) | By sector and direction, e.g. "Ex-India to Dubai" in INR, "Ex-Dubai to India" in AED | No |
| Lite | By Zone 1 to 9 (region names only, e.g. Zone 3 Middle East). Bookings in other currencies use USD rates | Yes |
| Flexi Plus, UpFront, Super 6E, Stretch, Stretch+ | Sector tables on their own pages | No |
| Codeshare | By zone, in USD | Yes |
| Domestic | One table, with equivalent amounts in other currencies | No |

International changes and cancellations close 4 hours before departure; domestic, 3 hours. IndiGo's page shows its tables in tabs, so a single read can miss a fare: reading several IndiGo pages, plus the airline's fee sheet for zone mapping, is safer.

## Not suitable for website reading

- **Airblue (PA):** no amounts published; the fee is printed on the e-ticket.
- **Full-service airlines** (Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad, Gulf Air, Oman Air): penalties depend on the fare basis and come with the booking through GDS or NDC fare rules, not the website.
